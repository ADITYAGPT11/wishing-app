# Global/Western digital invitation, e-card and group-card competitors (as of 2026-11-14)

Research note: about 23 search/fetch calls. Many vendor pricing pages could not be fetched directly (GroupGreeting returned 403), and many search results were competitor-marketing pages (Greenvelope, RSVPify, usecarly, party.pro), so their figures are marked as such. Where coverage was thin (Adobe Express, Smilebox, Postable, Punchbowl detail, Posh, Howdy, Google), this is said in the Gaps sections, not filled from memory.

## Product profiles: what each product does, its features, pricing, platform and recent changes

### Takeaway
The market splits into four groups. (1) RSVP-first event apps: Partiful, Apple Invites, Luma, Snapchat Plans, WhatsApp Events. They have no or low fees and a curated look. (2) Designed-invitation platforms: Evite, Paperless Post/Flyer, Greetings Island, Punchbowl. They charge per event, per guest or by subscription, with ads on the free tier. (3) General design tools: Canva, Adobe Express. They have strong editors and weak or no native RSVP. (4) E-card and group-card sites: Jacquie Lawson, American Greetings/Blue Mountain, Hallmark, Kudoboard, GroupGreeting. They sell subscriptions or charge per card and have no event loop. Big platforms (Apple, Snap, Meta/WhatsApp) moved into event invites in 2024-2026.

## Which handle the full loop: remember occasion → create → send → RSVP → reminders?

### Takeaway
No competitor found covers the full loop, especially the "remember occasion" step (birthday or anniversary reminders that prompt you to create something). Event apps (Partiful, Apple Invites, Evite, Paperless Post, WhatsApp Events, Luma) cover create → send → RSVP → reminders or updates. E-card sites cover create → send, with at most scheduled delivery. Group-card tools cover collaborative create → send.

### Cited Findings
- WhatsApp Events: create → share link → RSVP (yes, no or maybe, plus-one) → automatic reminder to "going" guests → pinned event — [WhatsApp blog](https://blog.whatsapp.com/new-feature-roundup-updates-to-group-chats-events-calls-channels-and-more); [Lowyat](https://www.lowyat.net/2024/321497/whatsapp-events-groups-communities/)
- Paperless Post: send by email, text or link → RSVP → unlimited broadcast messages (reminders, thank-yous) → guest check-in — [Paperless Post pricing](https://paperlesspost.com/pricing)
- Apple Invites: create → share link or iMessage → RSVP from any device → Shared Album after the event, plus polls, co-hosts and a widget — [MacRumors](https://macrumors.com/2026/11/11/apple-invites-gets-two-new-features); [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/04/23/apple-invites-for-iphone-adds-7-new-features-including-an-imessage-app/?extended-comments=1)
- Partiful: invite link → RSVP with a phone number → text blasts → shared photo album — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/1662982304); [party.pro](https://party.pro/partiful/)
- Snapchat Plans: create → invite friends → RSVP tracking, inside Snapchat only — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/10/snapchat-takes-aim-at-partiful-with-new-event-planning-features/)
- Canva: create only. RSVP needs a third-party app or form — [Jotform](https://www.jotform.com/blog/canva-rsvp/)
- EzInvito advertises RSVP with calendar sync — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/ezinvito/launches)
- "Invited" (Product Hunt) is an AI reminder scanner, the closest thing found to a "remember" layer — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/invited-ai-reminder-scanner)

### Inferences
- "Remember the occasion → nudge to wish or invite" looks like an open gap among Western invite tools. Calendar and contacts apps or Facebook birthday reminders fill that role, outside these products.
- Combining wishes (e-cards) with event invites in one product is uncommon. American Greetings and Greetings Island have both cards and invitations but weak RSVP depth. Paperless Post offers cards and invitations with strong RSVP, but in USD with coin friction.

### Gaps
- Calendar add (ICS or Google Calendar) support was not verified product by product, except EzInvito's claim.
- Whether American Greetings, Jacquie Lawson or Hallmark offer occasion reminders (birthday reminder emails) was not verified in this round.

## What customization approach do they use (full editor vs curated themes/effects)?

### Takeaway
There are three models. Full freeform editor: Canva, Adobe Express. Template plus constrained customization with paid upgrades: Paperless Post (paid envelope, liner, stamp and backdrop elements), Evite, Greetings Island. Curated themes and effects with minimal editing: Partiful, Apple Invites (backgrounds, Image Playground AI backgrounds, emoji effects, confetti). E-card sites (Jacquie Lawson, American Greetings) offer fixed animated cards plus a personal message. AI-generated invites are the 2025-2026 trend.

### Cited Findings
- Paperless Post monetizes customization elements one by one (envelope, liner, backdrop, stamp, custom colours, logo or sticker), each priced in coins — [Paperless Post pricing](https://paperlesspost.com/pricing)
- Apple Invites: curated backgrounds, Image Playground AI backgrounds (Oct 2026), drifting emoji effect (Nov 2026), confetti on RSVP (Jul 2026) — [MacRumors Nov](https://macrumors.com/2026/11/11/apple-invites-gets-two-new-features); [MacRumors Jul](https://macrumors.com/2026/07/21/apple-invites-app-two-new-features); [AppleWorld.Today](https://appleworld.today/2026/10/apple-updates-its-invites-app-to-take-advantage-of-new-ios-27-features/)

Greetings Island has an AI Invitation Generator. — [hunted.space](https://hunted.space/product/ainviter); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6741699223); [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/ai-invitation-generator)
- Jacquie Lawson: a fixed library of 400+ artist-made animated cards — [JL membership](https://www.jacquielawson.com/prices-membership)

### Inferences
- The winning consumer pattern (Partiful, Apple) is "curated effects + instant shareable link" rather than a full editor. That suits mobile-first, WhatsApp-sharing Indian users who want speed over design control.

### Gaps
- Music or audio support in invites (common in Indian video invites) was not verified for any Western player.
- Partiful's theme and effects catalogue was not detailed from a primary source.

## What are their monetization models and price points?

### Takeaway
The models are: free with ads (Evite free); per-event or per-guest (Evite Premium $17.99-$99.99, Paperless Post coins); subscriptions (Paperless Pro $250/year, Evite Pro $249.99/year, Greetings Island $2.25-$3.49/month, Punchbowl about $4-$16/month, American Greetings about $30-$36/year, Hallmark+ $79.99/year, Jacquie Lawson about $30-$36/year); per-card group cards (GroupGreeting about $4.99, Kudoboard from $5.99); ticketing fees (Luma 5% free tier, Partiful ticketing); and platform bundling (Apple via iCloud+ $0.99/month and up). Everything is priced in USD except Canva's INR plans.

### Cited Findings
- Evite: free with ads; Premium per event $17.99-$99.99 by guest tier; Pro $249.99/year — [usecarly](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/evite-pricing/); [party.pro](https://old.party.pro/?p=6197)

## What do users complain about (ads, guest signup friction, price)?

### Takeaway
Consistent complaints: ads on Evite's free invites, plus slow loads and RSVP bugs; confusing and fast-rising coin pricing on Paperless Post; guest friction from accounts or phone numbers (Partiful earlier required accounts; Apple Invites needed email verification codes until Sep 2026, and non-Apple guests often ignore the link); and price creep on Kudoboard. Reddit-specific evidence was not retrieved, and most complaint sources are competitor or affiliate blogs.

### Cited Findings
- Evite free version: guests see third-party ads. A review describes ads overwhelming the page and RSVPs not showing (2017 review). Others report slow load times and RSVP bugs. — [usecarly / party.pro roundup](https://old.party.pro/?p=6403); [SmartCustomer](https://www.smartcustomer.com/reviews/evite.com?page=3)
- Paperless Post: the coin model is "confusing and becomes expensive fast", and the cost is hard to know until checkout — [Greenvelope (competitor)](https://www.greenvelope.com/resources/best-evite-alternatives); [Trustpilot](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/paperlesspost.com)
- Partiful previously required guests to create an account to RSVP (2025) — [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/invites-is-apples-new-party-planning-app). It now uses phone-number entry (2026) — [party.pro](https://party.pro/partiful/). Low-reliability aggregator reports Android performance complaints — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.partiful.partiful)
- Apple Invites: email verification friction for guests was eased in Sep 2026 (tap a link instead of a code) — [MacRumors](https://macrumors.com/2026/09/30/apple-invites-app-three-new-features). Readers say non-Apple guests often reply by text instead — [MacRumors comments](https://macrumors.com/2026/11/11/apple-invites-gets-two-new-features)
- Kudoboard: "pricing has increased and can be quite limited in options" — [SourceForge review](https://sourceforge.net/software/product/Kudoboard)
- Canva: invitations lack RSVP and guest-list tools, so users stitch together forms — [1Invites (competitor)](https://blog.1invites.com/canva-vs-1invites-best-invitation-maker-choice/); [Jotform](https://www.jotform.com/blog/canva-rsvp/)
- WhatsApp Events: the plus-one option could not be disabled (beta-era report) — [PhoneArena](https://www.phonearena.com/news/whatsapp-is-widely-rolling-out-an-event-feature-for-group-chats_id159937)

### Inferences
- An India-first app should avoid ads on guest-facing invites, require no guest signup (open the link and RSVP by name), use transparent INR pricing, and render well on Android in WhatsApp's in-app browser. These are the main Western pain points.

### Gaps
- No Reddit threads were retrieved, so complaint evidence relies on review sites and competitor blogs (biased).
- App Store and Play Store rating figures for each app were not collected.
