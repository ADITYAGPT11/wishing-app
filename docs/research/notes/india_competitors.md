## Does any Indian product combine wishes + invites + RSVP + family occasion calendar (incl. tithi birthdays)?

### Takeaway
No. Across the searches, no product combined all four. The market is split into four groups: invite makers (no RSVP, no reminders), WhatsApp RSVP and guest tools (wedding-only, per-event fees), panchang and tithi-reminder apps (no invites or wishes), and wishes/photo-frame apps (no reminders or RSVP). The nearest partial combinations are Weddingkart (invites + WhatsApp RSVP, wedding-only) and Nutan Vedic Panchang or Tithi Tracker (tithi birthdays + reminders, no invites).

### Cited Findings
**Invites + RSVP (wedding-only)**

One-tap WhatsApp RSVP buttons with no app install. Guest lists imported from Excel and grouped by family side; mehendi, sangeet, wedding and reception share one master list. Personalized image, PDF and video invites with each guest's name in 11 Indian scripts. Voice-AI agent calls guests in 14 languages to collect RSVPs. Broadcasts reach 800 guests in 30 seconds. Pricing Basic ₹4,999 / Standard ₹8,499 per wedding + 18% GST; Enterprise custom for agencies with 10+ weddings a year. Self-reported 50,000+ guests across 100+ weddings. Bootstrapped. — [StartupTalky](https://startuptalky.com/weddingkart-success-story/)
- Weddingkart claims RSVP response rates of 70%+ vs 25–35% for web forms. Its Standard tier allows up to 4,500 messages per wedding, and a free tier offers about 30 WhatsApp messages per month (self-reported; The Quint piece is sponsored "brandstudio" content). — [The Quint (sponsored)](https://www.thequint.com/brandstudio/partner-weddingkart-ai-wedding-guest-management-india)

### Inferences
- The integrated wedge is open. Wishes, invites, RSVP and a family calendar with tithi birthdays are each served by separate apps, and none links them. Tithi-birthday reminders exist only as small single-purpose iOS apps. That makes them a plausible differentiator and a reason to return year-round, while wedding-RSVP tools see users once per wedding.
- Weddingkart is the closest threat on the RSVP side because it is WhatsApp-native and multilingual. Its per-wedding pricing (₹5k–₹8.5k) and wedding-only focus leave birthdays, pujas, griha pravesh and festivals uncovered.
- Meta AI and WhatsApp stickers make generic festive wishes free. A wishes product has to win on personalization (names, relationships, family data, timing) rather than on image generation alone.

## How do Indian users currently send invites (WhatsApp image/video/PDF) and what do they pay?

### Takeaway
Most invites go out on WhatsApp as a JPG, a multi-page PDF or a short MP4 (kept under about 16 MB). They are made with Canva or template apps (free to about ₹500) or bought from freelancers and studios (about ₹500–₹25,000 per design, with outliers higher). RSVP is rarely collected, because image, PDF and video invites have no RSVP mechanism.

### Cited Findings
- Canva invites go out as a photo or multi-page PDF over WhatsApp or email. Canva has no RSVP tracking; paid designs start at about ₹60 without a watermark. — [LBB Delhi](https://lbb.in/delhi/websites-wedding-invitations-online/)

- WedMeGood vendor "Electronic Invitations" lists video invites at about ₹100–₹200 per card with a 50-card minimum. Other vendors list ₹50–₹100 or ₹200–₹400 per card. — [WedMeGood vendor profile](https://www.wedmegood.com/profile/Electronic-Invitations-103430)

## What are common Play Store / Reddit complaints?

### Takeaway
Searches returned **no usable Reddit threads** (r/india, r/IndianWeddings, r/delhi). Complaint evidence comes from app-store listings and review aggregators. The recurring themes are paywalled watermark removal, aggressive subscriptions, payment problems, intrusive ads (calendar apps) and forced updates.

### Cited Findings
- An invitation-maker review quoted by an aggregator: "all of these invitation apps are garbage, and they try to trick you into spending money to remove the watermark or pay extra for better quality invitations" (single anecdotal review). — [JustUseApp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1458411327/invitation-maker-invite-maker/reviews)
- Feedback for "Invitation Maker: Card Creator" mentions "payment not reflecting" alongside generally positive reviews. — [Kimola report](https://kimola.com/reports/comprehensive-feedback-analysis-on-invitation-maker-app-google-play-en-144572)
- VIP paywalls (templates, watermark removal, envelopes) are standard in invitation apps. — [AppFollow](https://apps.appfollow.io/ios/invitation-maker-2024/1455444668?country=us)
- Kalnirnay app reviewers complain of intrusive ads while navigating dates, a forced update prompt that blocks use, no iOS widget and no offline use. — [App Store: Kalnirnay](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/id955413793)
- Older relatives often don't tap "RSVP" links. This is a vendor claim, uncited, from Weddingkart's own marketing. — [Weddingkart blog](https://www.weddingkart.co/blog/best-ai-wedding-invitation-generators)
- Paperless Post's coin system is "annoying" and its costs rise quickly with guest count (competitor blog). — [Weddingkart blog](https://www.weddingkart.co/blog/best-ai-wedding-invitation-generators)

### Inferences
- Users resent watermark and paywall tricks and weekly subscriptions. Transparent one-time or per-event pricing, or a generous free tier, is a likely trust lever.
- The big wishes and calendar apps rely on ads. An ad-light experience is a differentiator, and Drik Panchang's ₹199/year ad-removal price sets a low anchor for willingness to pay in the calendar category.

### Gaps
- No verified Reddit quotes; the searches returned none. A manual Reddit pass is recommended.
- No India-filtered Play Store low-star review sampling for Crafty Art, WedMeGood or wishes apps. Ratings for Crafty Art, WedMeGood and ShaadiSaga were not confirmed.

## How are multi-function weddings (Haldi, Mehendi, Sangeet, Reception) handled in digital invites today?

### Takeaway
There are three patterns: (1) one long multi-page PDF or video listing every function, sent to everyone; (2) separate image or video invites per function, sent manually to subsets of contacts; (3) a few platforms (Joy, Weddingkart, Paperless Post) that use a master list with per-event labels or pages and per-event RSVP. Pattern 3 is rare among India-based users and, outside Weddingkart, depends on email.

### Cited Findings
- Many couples send separate invitations for each event: mehndi, sangeet, ceremony and reception. — [Evite](https://www.evite.com/invites/wedding/wedding-weekend/indian-wedding)
- Most Indian weddings have distinct guest lists per event: haldi is limited to close family, sangeet is larger. A haldi message sent to the full list "causes confusion and hurt feelings". Joy recommends one master list with event labels rather than separate spreadsheets. It notes diaspora weddings see 5–10% of confirmed guests skip events and 3–7% of unconfirmed guests attend anyway. — [Joy blog](https://withjoy.com/blog/how-to-manage-multiple-wedding-events-with-different-guest-lists-for-indian-weddings/)
- WedMeGood suggests making separate videos for each function, personalized for the people invited to each. — [WedMeGood blog](https://www.wedmegood.com/blog/6-kinds-of-wedding-videos-to-make-including-the-2-minute-wedding-video-exclusively-for-whatsapp/)
- LBB advises choosing a template with room for the whole itinerary, i.e., one card for all functions. — [LBB](https://lbb.in/delhi/wedding-invites-save-the-46dfa2/)
- Paperless Post's Indian wedding section supports multiple pages for separate functions and a Ganesh Ji logo option. Canva produces multi-page PDFs (no RSVP). — [LBB Delhi](https://lbb.in/delhi/websites-wedding-invitations-online/)
- Weddingkart runs mehendi, sangeet, wedding and reception off one master guest list with WhatsApp RSVP. — [StartupTalky](https://startuptalky.com/weddingkart-success-story/)
- Minted sells matching mehndi and sangeet stationery sets ($300–$1,200 per suite). — [Weddingkart blog](https://www.weddingkart.co/blog/best-ai-wedding-invitation-generators)

### Inferences
- The unmet need for most Indian families: one event page with sub-events, each guest seeing only their functions (by tags), per-function RSVP collected in WhatsApp or with a one-tap link, and per-function reminders (venue, time, dress code) sent automatically before each function. Today this is either manual (separate WhatsApp sends) or a paid wedding-only tool.
- The NRI/diaspora angle: diaspora couples already use Joy, Zola or Paperless Post (email-first, per-event RSVP), but their India-based relatives are on WhatsApp. A hybrid with email for overseas guests and WhatsApp for Indian relatives, plus timezone-aware times, is a plausible gap. RSVPji (UK South Asian weddings) shows early diaspora-specific attempts.

### Gaps
- No quantitative data on what share of Indian weddings use separate per-function digital invites vs one combined card.
- Could not confirm whether WedMeGood's or ShaadiSaga's in-app tools support per-function guest tagging and RSVP.
